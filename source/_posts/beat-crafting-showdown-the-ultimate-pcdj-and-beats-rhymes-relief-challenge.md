## Beat Crafting Showdown - The Ultimate PCDJ & Beats Rhymes Relief Challenge!

[![](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/slider-contest31.jpg?resize=412%2C270&ssl=1)](https://i0.wp.com/pcdj.com/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/slider-contest31.jpg?fit=412%2C460&ssl=1 "slider-contest3")

PCDJ is a proud sponsor of Beats Rhymes & Relief, a non-profit organization utilizing the arts to raise awareness and support for worldwide humanitarian relief efforts. Beats, Rhymes & Relief harnesses the power of cultural diplomacy to raise the profile of global issues through high-profile public music concerts. Beats, Rhymes & Relief is a US based NGO headquartered in Washington, DC. 

[![slider-contest](https://i0.wp.com/beatsrhymesandrelief.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/slider-contest.jpg?resize=750%2C295)](https://i0.wp.com/beatsrhymesandrelief.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/09/slider-contest.jpg)  

**DC, NEW YORK, CHICAGO & LA**

****MC’s DJ’s Producers B-Boys & Girls Graffiti Artists**

**We’re kickin’ off the fall with 5 new music videos to raise social awareness and investment for our brothers and sisters in need. If you’re an MC, DJ, Producer, B-boy , B-girl or Graffiti Artist…. This is your chance to step up and put yourself out there and help out along the way. Our first two submissions are below from Dallas Texas and Chicago Illinois….. check them out first…. and if you think you can play in this game play the videos below to get the scoop.**

**DALLAS TEXAS ENTRY: ABOUT TIME**

**CHICAGO ENTRY: SHINE**

**THE SCOOP**

**In each city we will choose a crew of diverse artists to represent. This fall once all the videos are posted we will host a 3 day voting session where you and all your fans as well as ours can vote on which city put it down the best. At the end of the voting we will announce the name of the city that won and invite the entire crew to perform the track at our concert – opening up for our headliners. Our sponsors PCDJ ([www.pcdj.com](https://www.pcdj.com "www.pcdj.com")) will also be donating a copy of their PCDJ DEX 2 DJ and video mixing software and an American Audio VMS2 DJ controller (MAC & Windows), a retail value over $700**

**[![dex2withvms2lrg](https://i2.wp.com/beatsrhymesandrelief.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/dex2withvms2lrg.png?resize=434%2C271)](https://i2.wp.com/beatsrhymesandrelief.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/08/dex2withvms2lrg.png)**

**NOW THAT YOU KNOW THE DEAL – HERE’S WHAT WE NEED FROM YOU:**

****DJ’s – Cuts and drops for the track**

**MC’s – 8 bars for the track**

**Producers – sample of the best track you got**

**B-boys & B-girls of all ages – video of you gettin down**

**Graffiti Artists – pics of your best 3 tags**

**The themes are messages of peace, education and social investment. Using art to create a common ground and find solutions.**

**KEEP IT PG – NO RELIGION – NO POLITICS**

**TO ENTER, PLEASE VISIT [THIS PAGE](http://beatsrhymesandrelief.org/contest/) AND LOOK FOR THE SUBMISSION FORM AT THE BOTTOM – GOOD LUCK!**
